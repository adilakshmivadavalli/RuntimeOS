# BFSI governance research → RuntimeOS product mapping

The supplied research note is dated 3 October 2026.

- **Pages 1–4:** Pre-MCP permission gate; OFF/LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH governance levels; LOW baseline controls; MEDIUM contextual controls; HIGH harm/provenance/tool-metadata controls.
- **Pages 5–6:** B1–B3 banking, F1–F3 finance, I1–I3 insurance scenarios and benchmark catalogue.
- **Pages 8–13:** common adapter interface, OpenTelemetry trace design, ASR/PUA/NRP/FPR/escalation/latency metrics, calibration, least privilege, fairness and audit completeness.
- **Page 14:** build roadmap and caveats around external benchmark setup/licences and the non-availability of the LawZero Scientist AI model.

RuntimeOS implements the product-facing tiered gate, curated scenario replay, evidence and governance metrics. External benchmark adapters are contracts/documentation only until the respective public repositories are installed, validated and actually executed.
